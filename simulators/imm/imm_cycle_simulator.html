    Model basis

    Cooling time from the one-dimensional plate equation t = s²/(π²α)·ln[(8/π²)(Tmelt−Tmould)/(Teject−Tmould)]; gate freeze uses the same equation at 60% of wall. The set cooling time counts from the end of hold, so the calculated minimum is reduced by hold time and half the fill time. Injection power = injection pressure × injection rate (1 kW = 10 000 bar·cm³/s); when the drive cannot deliver it, fill speed drops. Theoretical shot volume = screw area × injection stroke. Clamp force = projected area × cavity pressure (scaled with flow length / wall ratio) + 10% for runners; the shot also carries a 10% runner allowance. SEC follows the EUROMAP 60.1 idea of machine-only energy per kg of shot (no dryer, chiller or mould temperature unit), built from drive base load, motion energy, injection work and melt enthalpy. Material constants, drive efficiencies and flow-pressure factors are generic rules of thumb. Treat the numbers as estimates for comparing settings, then confirm on the machine with a metered SEC test.
